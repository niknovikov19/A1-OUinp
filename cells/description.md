Old TC and HTC models that were used in this repo since the major revision are now moved to `nki_a1_samn` subfolder. 
They originate from: `https://github.com/NathanKlineInstitute/A1/tree/samn`
These TC and HTC are identical (they shouldn't) and produce bad V-I curves.

Current TC and HTC versions are taken from: `https://github.com/smcelroy97/A1/tree/main`
Same parameters are in the A1 paper repo: `https://github.com/NathanKlineInstitute/Macaque_auditory_thalamocortical_model_data`
